# Lux-Tech-Week-1-Excel-project

![](https://github.com/MastingoJay/Lux-Tech-Week-1-Excel-project/blob/main/Screenshot%20(247).png)

This project is about analysing and understanding sales performance using Excel. It includes cleaning raw data, doing calculations, creating Pivot Tables, charts, and building a full interactive dashboard.

# 1.Data Cleaning

- Converted sales_date to proper date format

- Ensured sales_value and sales_quantity are numeric

- Removed or fixed missing/invalid entries

- Structured the data into a clean, organized format for analysis

# 2.Basic Statistical Analysis

- Total Sales Value: Sum of all sales revenue

- Average Sales Value: Average amount per transaction

- Total Quantity Sold: Sum of all units sold

- Average Quantity Sold: Average units per sale

# 3. Data Analysis

- Sales by Region: To see which parts of the world are bringing in the most revenue.

- Sales by Channel: Comparing how much money came in from different sales methods like In-store, Online, or Distributor.

- Sales by Salesperson: Tracks how individual sales reps performed,useful for performance reviews or reward systems.

# 4. Sales Dashboard Sheet

- Bar Charts showing sales by region and channel.

- Line Chart tracking monthly sales trends across the year.

- Pie Chart showing sales distribution across regions.

- Salesperson Performance Chart visualizing quantity and value of sales by each salesperson.

- Slicers (filters): Allow you to filter and view data for specific regions, channels, salespeople, or dates with a single click.

# 5. Key Findings

- Top Regions: North America and South America had the highest sales.

- Top Channel: In-store sales performed best, followed by Direct Sales. Distributors did poorly.

- Sales Trend: Sales were not stable month-to-month — some months had major drops.

- Underperforming Areas: Asia, Africa, and Europe had lower sales compared to others.

# 6. Recommendations

- Focus more on North & South America to grow sales further.

- Boost In-store sales through better promotions and customer experience.

- Investigate and improve the Distributor channel with training or incentives.

- Create strategies to stabilize monthly sales, such as seasonal offers and loyalty programs.

- Study and grow sales in Asia, Europe, and Africa through marketing or new partnerships.
